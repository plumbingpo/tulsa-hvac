Keeping Your Norman HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a miserable night.
